## Brand & Style

This design system blends **Dark Modern Editorial** with **High-Contrast Cinematic Minimalism**. It is tailored for high-end digital design and creative direction portfolios, commanding instant authority through massive condensed typography, tactile obsidian surfaces, and sharp crimson red focal points.

### Personality & Values
- **Monolithic & Confident:** Bold display scales evoke high-fashion editorial layouts and luxury cinematic posters rather than generic tech dashboards.
- **Architectural Precision:** Every module adheres to rigorous grid lines, fine 1px structural strokes, and balanced negative space.
- **Dynamic Drama:** Deep obsidian blacks paired with saturated crimson gradients provide atmospheric depth, glowing callouts, and clear visual pacing.

### Design Aesthetic
The system draws primarily from **High-Contrast Modernism** fused with subtle **Ambient Tonal Layering**. Flat content cards are anchored by translucent structural borders, restrained neon-like crimson backlights, and sharp contrast between warm metallic greys and stark white type.

## Colors

The palette operates in a permanent dark mode built on deep carbon tones, highlighted by intense crimson and disciplined neutrals.

### Palette Architecture
- **Obsidian Core (Canvas & Surfaces):**
  - Base Canvas: `#0A0A0A`
  - Elevated Container Tier 1: `#121212`
  - Elevated Container Tier 2: `#1A1A1A`
  - Deep Crimson Ambient Basin: `#4A080C`
- **Signal & Accents:**
  - Primary Crimson: `#D91E2A` (used for active states, key metric highlights, numbers, callout badges, and glowing borders)
  - Crimson Hover / Flare: `#FF3342`
  - Crimson Ambient Shadow: `rgba(217, 30, 42, 0.25)`
- **Editorial Typography & Dividers:**
  - High-Contrast Off-White: `#F5F5F5` (Headings, hero titles, primary action text)
  - Neutral Reading Grey: `#9A9A9A` (Body paragraphs, sub-headers)
  - Meta Dark Grey: `#5E5E5E` (Secondary metrics, structural labels, disabled icons)
  - Structural Ghost Border: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`
  - Active Glow Border: `rgba(217, 30, 42, 0.5)`

## Typography

The typography system relies on an intentional dynamic contrast: monolithic, tall condensed display sans (`Anton`) juxtaposed against a clean, humanist geometric body workhorse (`Plus Jakarta Sans`). 

### Type Roles
- **Hero & Numerical Display (`Anton`):** Used strictly uppercase for hero impact, massive index identifiers (`01`, `02`, `03`), and key statistical figures (`40+`, `3+`).
- **Section Headers & UI Copy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers clarity and balanced rhythm across body text, module titles, and interactive components.
- **Micro Headings (`label-caps`):** Set in full uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.14em`) to establish crisp architectural category tags, status updates, and breadcrumbs.
- **Accents:** Personal greetings, quotes, and authentic signatures should leverage an expressive, human script like `Caveat` to humanize the stark brutalist framework.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by an asymmetric **Bento Grid Architecture** on a 12-column grid system, emphasizing full-bleed sections segmented by fine structural line divisions.

### Grid & Responsiveness
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `3rem` canvas margins, and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers typically span 4, 6, 8, or 12 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 6-column fluid grid, `2rem` outer margins, `1rem` gutters. Multi-column cards collapse to 3-column pairs or stacked rows.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid, `1.25rem` outer margins, `1rem` gutters. Bento containers reflow to full-width cards in single vertical stacks.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical cadence relies on generous rhythm steps (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) between high-level editorial sections, while compact component spacing (`space-xs` through `space-md`) ensures compact density within list groups, tech pills, and metadata strips.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use diffuse, cloudy drop shadows; instead, it combines **tonal layering**, **structural ghost lines**, and **crimson directional luminance**.

### Tiers of Depth
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#0A0A0A` pure obsidian ground.
- **Level 1 (Bento Surfaces):** `#121212` with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Floating Panels):** `#1A1A1A` with a 1px border of `rgba(217, 30, 42, 0.5)` and a directional floor ambient glow: `0px 12px 32px rgba(217, 30, 42, 0.15)`.
- **Level 3 (Accented Editorial Blocks):** Linear gradients spanning from `#4A080C` down to `#121212` at an angle of 145 degrees, creating warm cinematic spotlight zones behind critical quotes or personal testimonials.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses **Soft (Level 1)** geometric cuts. It maintains high-precision, architectural rectilinearity with subtle, unobtrusive corner softeners (`0.25rem` / `4px`) for primary cards and modular containers, avoiding bubbly interfaces.

### Specialty Radii
- **Base Cards & Modules:** `rounded` (4px / 0.25rem) or `rounded-lg` (8px / 0.5rem) for clean framing.
- **Interactive Action Tags & Utility Chips:** Fully rounded pills (`9999px`) to create an unmistakable contrast between the structural grid blocks and touch targets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Crimson `#D91E2A` fill, `#F5F5F5` bold uppercase text, pill shape (`9999px`), padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Hover state raises surface brightness to `#FF3342` with an ambient glow (`0 0 20px rgba(217, 30, 42, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary Ghost Pill:** Transparent fill, 1px border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.24)`, `#F5F5F5` text. On hover: border switches to `rgba(217, 30, 42, 0.8)` with crimson-tinted text.
- **Icon Action Buttons:** Circular (`40px x 40px`), `#1A1A1A` fill, centered 16px monochrome icon.

### Project & Bento Cards
- **Container:** `#121212` surface, 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), `0.5rem` border radius, padding `1.5rem`.
- **Interactive State:** Border shifts smoothly to `rgba(217, 30, 42, 0.5)` with cursor arrow transition.
- **Numeric Badging:** Preceded by large `Anton` index badges (e.g., `01`, `02`) rendered in `#D91E2A` with an adjacent directional arrow link.

### Chips & Skill Badges
- **Style:** Compact pill badge (`rounded-full`), `#1A1A1A` background, 1px border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Typography:** `label-caps` in `#9A9A9A`, transitioning to `#F5F5F5` on hover.

### Process & Stepper Lists
- **Structure:** Connected vertical nodes via a 1px vertical spine (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- **Step Indicators:** Circular `#121212` badges housing an icon or step counter with 1px border in `#D91E2A`.

### Testimonial & Quote Panels
- **Surface:** Angular background gradient (`#4A080C` to `#121212`), high-contrast quotation glyphs rendered in translucent crimson, ending in authentic handwritten script signatures.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Flat `#121212` background, 1px border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`, `0.25rem` radius, padding: `0.75rem 1rem`.
- **Focus:** Crisp 1px border in `#D91E2A` accompanied by `0 0 12px rgba(217, 30, 42, 0.25)` outer glow.
